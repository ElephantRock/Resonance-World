# Resonance World — v0.1 Research Program

> **Direction note (2026-08-14):** this document remains the historical v0.1 World program and its experiment records are not rewritten. The current program direction broadens the product unit from recruited agents/organizations to **accessible, composable, reproducible, and regenerative capabilities developed across evolving AI environments**. See `docs/capability-development-v0.1.md` for the current capability-development model and `docs/mechanism-governance-v0.1.md` for prospective statistical, novelty, treatment-integrity, and registry-promotion governance. Existing W/O/H evidence remains valid only within its registered scopes.

## Research boundary

Resonance Field continues independently as the laboratory for within-community emergence.

Resonance World begins only when multiple independently developing Fields are connected through explicit federation contracts.

The World program must not require altering Field outcomes merely to make inter-field behavior easier to observe.

## Primary hypothesis

> Cross-field organizations recruited from independently developed societies can assemble transferable capability that outperforms equally resourced purpose-built organizations on unseen missions, while preserving the productive diversity of their source Fields.

This hypothesis has two mandatory components:

1. **organizational advantage** — recruited cross-field organizations must produce measurable mission value;
2. **ecological sustainability** — recruitment must not merely strip source communities of the capability required to continue adapting.

## Experimental phases

### Phase W0 — Contract validation

Goal: prove that a World can observe and coordinate multiple Fields without importing their internal implementation.

Topology:

- 3 Fields;
- 20 active agents per Field;
- no inter-field contact;
- standardized field identity and capability export only.

Questions:

- Can Fields be registered and observed independently?
- Are passports reproducible from evidence?
- Can local and portable capability be represented separately?
- Are provenance boundaries preserved?

Success gate:

World integration produces no change in isolated Field behavior under matched controls beyond instrumentation overhead.

### Phase W1 — Transfer tests

Goal: distinguish home-ecology fitness from portable capability.

Intervention:

Selected agents perform bounded tasks in unfamiliar evaluation environments without permanently leaving their home Field.

Compare:

- high local reputation agents;
- high specialization agents;
- broad generalists;
- randomly selected controls.

Primary metrics:

- zero-shot success;
- adaptation latency;
- calibration drift;
- performance loss relative to home environment;
- dependence on exported memory;
- dependence on familiar collaborators.

### Phase W2 — Individual recruitment

Goal: test whether evidence-backed recruitment beats purpose-built selection.

Topology:

- 3 source Fields;
- 1 Organization;
- one unseen mission family.

Arms:

1. purpose-built baseline agents;
2. random cross-field recruits;
3. locally highest-reputation recruits;
4. highest portable-capability recruits.

Hard control:

Underlying models, tool access, total compute budget, and mission information must be matched.

### Phase W3 — Team recruitment

Goal: determine whether relationships themselves are transferable assets.

Candidate units:

- individually strong agents assembled ad hoc;
- recurring within-field teams;
- cross-field teams selected for complementarity;
- random teams.

Define provisional team capital:

```text
team_capital = observed_team_performance - expected_performance_from_members
```

Test whether positive team capital persists under transfer.

### Phase W4 — Persistent organization

Goal: determine whether an Organization can acquire capability independent of current membership.

Add:

- corporate memory;
- organization-level provenance;
- recurring recruitment;
- member turnover;
- multiple missions.

Questions:

- Does organizational memory improve newcomers?
- Can the organization survive founder/high-performer departure?
- Does corporate memory become overly dependent on one source Field?

### Phase W5 — Inter-field mobility

Goal: measure effects of talent movement on source communities.

Introduce:

- secondment;
- temporary migration;
- permanent migration;
- return migration.

Metrics:

- source-field task quality;
- specialization recovery;
- successor formation;
- knowledge retained after departure;
- knowledge returned after migration;
- time to replace lost capability.

This phase distinguishes **brain drain** from **brain circulation**.

### Phase W6 — Multiple organizations

Goal: introduce simultaneous cooperation and competition.

Topology target after earlier gates pass:

- 12 Fields;
- 24 active agents per Field;
- 3 Organizations;
- multiple mission families;
- bounded inter-field exchange.

Study:

- talent competition;
- organization specialization;
- source-field concentration;
- team poaching;
- knowledge spillovers;
- institutional persistence;
- market concentration.

### Phase W7 — World holdout

Goal: test generalization of the entire recruitment-and-organization mechanism.

Holdout must include:

- unseen mission family;
- unseen source-field combination;
- unseen environmental shock;
- independent seeds;
- no adaptive tuning after unblinding.

## Baselines

Every major World claim should be compared against at least:

- purpose-built single-agent baseline;
- purpose-built multi-agent baseline;
- random recruitment;
- local-reputation recruitment;
- portable-capability recruitment;
- recruited teams where applicable.

## Causal separations

The research program should avoid conflating:

- individual capability with team synergy;
- local status with portable competence;
- migration effects with knowledge-export effects;
- corporate memory with repeated member selection;
- organization advantage with additional compute;
- world connectivity with mere access to more information.

Use ablations to isolate each mechanism.

## World health constraints

A World-level mechanism is not successful merely because the best Organization improves.

Track at minimum:

- median source-field performance;
- worst-decile source-field performance;
- between-field capability diversity;
- concentration of top portable talent;
- organization hiring concentration;
- replacement/succession latency;
- retained public knowledge after departures.

A candidate mechanism that wins by permanently collapsing source-field diversity should be recorded as an extraction regime, not a successful world ecology.

## Initial implementation order

2. Evidence-backed Agent Passport schema.
3. Read-only capability discovery adapter for Resonance Field.
4. Transfer-test harness.
5. Organization and Mission schemas.

9. World metrics/evidence tables.
