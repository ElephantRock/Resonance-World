## W8-01 — Source reserve + composition efficiency: FAIL / REPLICATED

Primary cap: two active external rights per 12-agent source Field.

Discovery:

- unrestricted organization success: **55.1432%**

- cap-2 organization success: **57.2266%**
- cap-2 mean source loss: **4.2726 pp**
- source-loss-matched composition-efficiency cost: **0.7161 pp**
- gate: **FAIL**

Replication:

- unrestricted organization success: **46.8750%**

- cap-2 organization success: **48.1771%**
- cap-2 mean source loss: **3.5869 pp**
- source-loss-matched composition-efficiency cost: **4.7526 pp**

The cap is therefore not automatically source-protective. By changing which agents are
won, cap 2 selected smaller-but-more-source-critical sets in these populations and
increased measured source loss despite modest organization gains.

Cap 1 did protect source more strongly, but at a large organization-composition cost:

- discovery source loss **1.8288 pp**, organization success **29.2318%**;
- replication source loss **2.1576 pp**, organization success **31.1849%**.

This supports an explicit protection/composition trade-off, not a sustainable-cap claim.

## W8-05 — Integrated R+C+D+K charter: FAIL / REPLICATED

Discovery unrestricted baseline organization success: **80.7454%**.

Full charter:

- organization success: **69.6777%**
- mean source loss: **1.0759 pp**
- coalition effect: **+5.3060 pp**
- gate: **FAIL**

Replication unrestricted baseline organization success: **79.9859%**.

Full charter:

- organization success: **68.5384%**
- mean source loss: **0.6097 pp**
- coalition effect: **+4.8828 pp**
- gate: **FAIL**

The full institution successfully reduces source loss below 2 pp and preserves coalition
surplus, but organization service falls by roughly **11 pp** in both phases.

Leave-one-out evidence localizes the trade-off:

- removing circulation (`R+D+K`) raises organization success by **+11.77 pp** in discovery
  and **+11.55 pp** in replication, but increases source loss by **+3.20 pp** and
  **+2.98 pp** respectively;
- removing the dividend (`R+C+K`) changes no measured charter outcome;
- removing coalition coordination (`R+C+D`) changes no measured charter outcome.

Thus circulation is doing the source-protection work, and it is also the dominant cause
of the organization-viability failure.

- replication ends at alpha **38**, beta **500**, gamma **500**, with materially higher
