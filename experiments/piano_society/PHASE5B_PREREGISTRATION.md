## Institutional model memory

The institution fits an equal-prior posterior over two generic structural hypotheses from pre-turnover organization-owned episodes:

- `role_specific`: success is explained by distinct lead-skill and support-skill role competence.
- `cross_coverage`: success is explained by both selected members covering both mission skills.

For every paired unit, formation uses 96 rounds × two routing strategies = **192 pre-turnover episodes**. After formation, 100% of organization members are replaced.

Both arms then compute strategy forecasts on the **same replacement roster**:

- `model_retained` uses the posterior learned from the 192 pre-turnover episodes.
- `model_reset` uses the exact neutral posterior `{role_specific: 0.5, cross_coverage: 0.5}` with zero evidence episodes.

Thus the reset arm is not deprived of current member capability. It receives the same roster and the same forecaster, but no inherited structural evidence.

The environment never reads institutional memory or posterior state. Memory can affect outcomes only through the PIANO controller's routing choice.

## Confirmatory units and outcome law

There are **24 paired units**: six untouched confirmatory organizations × four missions. Each arm receives the exact same replacement roster and the exact same 128 common-random-number environment trial seeds. Evaluation does not update institutional memory.

Primary outcome: mean mission success rate over the 128 registered trials.

Secondary outcomes include forecast fidelity, whether the two arms choose different actions, cross-channel contradiction, intent/action divergence, report mismatch, unsupported success claims, token use, and model latency.

## Frozen advancement gate

Phase 5B advances only if **all** conditions hold:

1. mean `model_retained - model_reset` mission-success effect is at least **+0.03**;
2. exact paired two-sided sign-test p-value is at most **0.05**;
3. at least **4/6** field-level mean effects are nonnegative;
4. retained-minus-reset outcome-report-mismatch delta is at most **+0.05**;
5. each arm has at least **8 decisive forecast units** (forecast gap > `1e-12`);
6. each arm follows its own forecast-preferred strategy in at least **90%** of decisive units.

No post-hoc exclusions are allowed. A transport failure before complete artifact creation yields no scientific result. Once any complete Phase-5B confirmatory artifact exists, no frozen parameter above may be changed.
