## Decision-relevant constructor

A candidate is eligible only if all hold on the replacement roster:

- `specialist` and `balanced` route to different ordered member assignments;
- under the `role_specific` structural hypothesis, specialist exceeds balanced by at least 0.02;
- under `cross_coverage`, balanced exceeds specialist by at least 0.02;
- the neutral 0.5/0.5 structural forecast has margin at least 0.0005;
- the pre-turnover roster has structural-hypothesis identifiability at least 0.005.

The target environment structure is then chosen so its optimal policy **opposes the neutral-prior preference**. This is intentionally a capability/stress test, not an estimate of natural mission prevalence.

The confirmatory target composition is 5 `role_specific` / 7 `cross_coverage`, one unit per distinct organization.

## Memory intervention and PIANO controller

Each confirmatory organization forms 192 pre-turnover episodes, then undergoes 100% membership replacement.

- `model_retained` carries the posterior over `role_specific` versus `cross_coverage` inferred from those 192 organization-owned episodes.
- `model_reset` receives the exact neutral 0.5/0.5 prior with zero evidence episodes.

Both arms recompute the two routing forecasts on the **same current replacement roster**. The environment never reads institutional memory directly. Memory may affect outcomes only through the PIANO controller's routing decision.

Model-visible context contains opaque route IDs, skill aliases `skill-a` through `skill-f`, current roster capabilities under those aliases, the structural posterior, and the current-roster routing forecasts. Real skill names, hidden regime, constructor target hypothesis, target policy, and neutral-preferred-policy answer key are audit-only.

The fixed model is `glm-5.2` through the existing hardened Z.AI coding Chat Completions transport. Thinking is disabled, `do_sample=false`, temperature is 0, provider seed is unavailable, and each arm/unit uses exactly four logical PIANO calls: intention, speech, action, post-action report. Physical retries are transport events, not scientific observations.

## Confirmatory outcome law

There are **12 paired units**, one per selected fresh organization. Each arm uses the exact same replacement roster and the exact same 256 common-random-number environment trial seeds. Evaluation does not update institutional memory.

Presentation order is counterbalanced:

- specialist-first / balanced-first: 6/6;
- retained-first / reset-first: 6/6;
- the two factors cross at 3 units per cell.

Primary metric: mean mission success rate over the 256 registered trials.

## Frozen advancement gate

Phase 5C advances only if **all** conditions hold:

1. mean `model_retained - model_reset` mission-success effect is at least **+0.03**;
2. exact paired two-sided sign-test p-value is at most **0.05**;
3. at least **9/12** paired unit effects are nonnegative;
4. retained-minus-reset outcome-report-mismatch delta is at most **+0.05**;
5. reset forecast reproduces the constructor's neutral-preferred policy in **12/12** units;
6. retained posterior matches the registered target structural hypothesis in at least **10/12** units;
7. retained forecast selects the registered target policy in at least **10/12** units;
8. retained versus reset forecast preference changes in at least **10/12** units;
9. each arm has at least **10 decisive forecast units**;
10. each arm's controller follows its own forecast-preferred policy in at least **90%** of decisive units.

No post-hoc exclusions are allowed. A transport failure before complete artifact creation yields no scientific result. The exact unchanged complete-case job may be rerun after a transport-only failure. No frozen scientific parameter may change once Phase 5C model-backed inference begins.
