# C3R-G2 prospective design worksheet (NOT a preregistered experiment)

Status: **DRAFT / NOT FROZEN / NOT EXECUTION AUTHORIZATION**.
No inferential claim can be based on this document. The construction test fixture and proposed manipulations do not supply calibrated effect sizes or sample sizes.

## Mechanism identity and prior boundary

- Proposed mechanism: organization-owned executable workflow procedure learned from independent source-episode outcome evidence.
- Parent motivation: W5A organizational routing failed and H8 found role synthesis mattered; this study is therefore **posthoc-motivated** until independently justified and prospectively registered under mechanism-governance-v0.1.
- Distinct intervention: a context-conditional sequence of executable workflow actions inferred from prior evaluator-graded source experience, not a context-indexed pair-routing strategy and not a representational rewrite of the same raw history.
- No W5A, W5B, H8, or D2 outcomes are reused as new discovery evidence.

## Questions requiring calibration before a confirmatory freeze

1. Task and evaluator: establish opaque, previously unseen task rules; exhibit genuine uncertainty for baseline agents, and measurable acquisition by source agents before any organizational test.
2. Unit: determine the randomized independent organization or source-development replicate. Account for organization/Field-level correlation; mission count is not inferential N when organizations share development state.
3. Arms: A independent budget-matched attempts, B roles and non-learned procedure, C same underlying experience as raw history with equal interpretation opportunity, D compiled procedure, plus D-minus-procedure ablation.
4. Primary estimand: paired intention-to-treat success difference D minus C on disjoint held-out task units, with independent organizational development units. Secondary D minus B and D minus A and ablation are an explicitly corrected family.
5. Outcomes: externally graded binary success; provenance integrity, exposed development budget, training/compilation cost, per-arm calls/tokens, response failures, source capability loss are independently reported.
6. Assignment: specify source/roster formation, context allocation, paired outcome seeds, and independent evaluator lock. No treatment indicator, procedure ID, or memory object may enter truth law.
7. Sample planning: estimate expected discordance, intracluster correlation, provider analyzable yield by schema, candidate abstention rate, and minimum analyzable organizational units before choosing N.
8. Materiality: derive minimal important improvement from mission value and cost, or explicitly classify an arbitrary numeric decision threshold as conventional. Do not reuse the 2-episode construction floor.
9. Confirmatory statistics: predeclare alpha, familywise multiplicity procedure, paired/cluster-aware test, confidence-interval procedure, target power, sensitivity to information assumptions, stopping and missing-unit policy.
10. Integrity: identify source-only training cohort, separately locked heldout contexts/tasks, non-overlapping member identifiers, independent cohort after discovery for replication, and commitments to code hashes.
11. Treatment checks: prove equal access/budgets, reversible/identical nonsemantic evidence transforms, and no outcome/authority backdoor. Distinct development and holdout locks alone do not establish non-overlap of task semantic content.
12. Lifecycle: construction tests -> developmental feasibility -> prospective freeze -> independently authorized provider (if needed) -> heldout confirmatory result -> independent fresh replication -> separate acceptance decision.

## Advancement rules

- If a source agent fails to acquire the prerequisite ability under the new schema, stop at G1; never interpret a downstream organizational null as a valid test of an acquired capability.
- If acquisition passes but procedure compilation abstains, report abstention/yield honestly; do not select only favorable contexts after outcomes are observed.
- If D is not better than fair C/B controls on the frozen criteria, preserve the null/negative result. Do not retroactively change the compiler, thresholds, or comparator.
- If G2 is supported, start a separate prospective G3 study measuring complete turnover and independent knowledge use; G4 production and net regeneration remain out of scope.
