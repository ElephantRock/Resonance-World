## Purpose

H4 moves one causal step beyond the deterministic H1–H3 mechanism campaign. The decision-maker is now a fresh model call rather than a hand-coded support counter. Every call receives only its current task and the bounded evidence delivered by its registered treatment arm.

Production/default Historical Substrate remains disabled regardless of H4 outcome.

## Confirmatory decision rule

Four paired one-sided exact discordance tests are evaluated over `(unit, replicate)` cells: structured accumulating `g3` against flat `g3`, static `g3`, no-history `g3`, and structured accumulating `g1`. Holm family-wise error control is fixed at 0.05. Every contrast must have positive observed difference and survive Holm correction.

In addition, structured accumulating `g3` must strictly exceed flat accumulating `g3` in each replicate cohort separately.

These are evidentiary/semantic criteria under #118, not minimum-effect materiality claims.
