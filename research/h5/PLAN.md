# H5 — Institutional Mediation of Structured Organizational History

Preregistration: #152  
Frozen World base: `935e0463acc88f7f7756861d734eeba7b4efb034`  
ContextGraph release: `b896891108fd954869a8cd0423f6e8440ab0cdc0`  
Branch: `experiment/h5-institutional-mediation`

H5 holds the accepted H4 structured-history mechanism fixed and asks whether a frozen institutional protocol adds causal value beyond an equal-model-compute direct successor group with the same canonical organizational information.

The confirmatory panel contains 12 opaque units, three complete-turnover generations, three stochastic replicate cohorts, four arms, and exactly three model calls per organizational decision cell (two analysts plus one chair), for 432 organizational cells and 1,296 logical `glm-5-turbo` calls before transport retries.

Arms are `equal_compute_direct`, `roles_only`, `governed_static`, and `governed_persistent`. All arms receive the same six canonical records per unit/generation at the organization level. Role routing and a deterministic organization-owned routine digest may change representation and allocation, but not scientific information. The digest is reconstructible from those same public records.

The three challenge families are `cross_role_composition`, `authority_conflict`, and `routine_transfer`. Authority is orthogonal to evaluator truth: the World `AuthorityLedger` authorizes an opaque execution capability, while the evaluator separately scores the chosen decision action. Historical evidence and institutional state cannot create authority.

Primary g3 contrasts are governed-persistent versus direct, governed-static, and roles-only. Each uses a one-sided exact paired discordance test, with Holm FWER control at 0.05 over the three tests. All three differences must be positive and Holm-significant. Persistent must also beat direct separately in all three replicate cohorts, and must beat static on the `routine_transfer` family at g3.

All Gates 0–15 in #152 must pass for classification `historical_substrate_institutional_mediation_pass`. Any failed gate classifies the frozen campaign as `historical_substrate_institutional_mediation_failed`; confirmatory outcomes may not be used to retune the frozen apparatus.

Production/default Historical Substrate remains disabled regardless of outcome.
