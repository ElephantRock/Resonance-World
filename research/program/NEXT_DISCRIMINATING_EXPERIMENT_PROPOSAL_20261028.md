Status: **PROPOSAL ONLY — not frozen, not dispatched, zero sends.**
Prepared under the Research Program Realignment directive (2026-10-28).
This document proposes ONE experiment whose primary difference between
arms is an ARCHITECTURAL MECHANISM, not model capability.

## 0. Why this experiment (grounded in the evidence map)

Three findings from the full-repository review converge on one
unresolved discriminating question:

1. **Raw structured history works, and survives turnover by itself.**
   H1–H4 (confirmatory, replicated; H4 on a live model): an accumulating
   structured history channel causally improves decisions of both fixed
   controllers and fresh stochastic successors across five generations
   of complete member replacement.
2. **No mechanism layered OVER that history has yet proven added
   value.** H5 (mediation bundle: negative confirmatory); H8 (History
   IR and compiled semantic state: no aggregate improvement; only
   role/chair synthesis +8.33pp positive); H6/H7 (relevance-gated
   state: contradictory across fresh units).
3. **The organizational line never tested an evidence channel of that
   kind.** W5A tested its own routing-statistics/archive
   representations (null, replicated); W5B value is locked to intact
   pair membership. Nobody has measured, in an organizational setting,
   whether a **compiled, organization-owned procedure** derived from
   externally admitted graded evidence beats members' direct use of the
   SAME evidence.

The repo already contains a reviewed construction for exactly this
comparison — `docs/c3r-g2-construction.md` (C3R-G2, "causal
organizational capability foundation"), including its honest fixture
probe, its A/B/C/D arm design, and its known failure modes. It was
never run because its pre-flight required "a demonstrated
source-learning prerequisite" — a model-induction capability that the
acquisition program just closed as operationally unattainable at the
tested operating points. **The realignment decision removes that
blocker the right way:** the owner's directive replaces model
self-acquisition with **externally admitted, evaluator-graded,
identical evidence for all arms**. The source-learning prerequisite
becomes an evidence-admission protocol, not a model capability.

## 1. Research question (estimand)

> Under a fixed model, fixed resources, fixed tasks and an IDENTICAL
> corpus of evaluator-graded development evidence available to every
> arm, does executing an organization-level **compiled workflow
> procedure** improve fresh-member task performance beyond (a)
> budget-matched independent agents, (b) the same organization with a
> fixed non-learned procedure, and (c) the same organization with
> equivalent direct access to the raw evidence?

Primary estimand: paired performance difference D−C (compiled procedure
vs equal-information raw history) on a held-out task set, executed by
FRESH members who did not participate in development. Secondary
estimands: D−B (learning component), D−A (organizational usefulness
baseline), and a pre-declared procedure-removal condition (D without
the procedure, matched neutral replacement) to test that the procedure
— not some correlate — carries any effect.

## 2. Arms (from the frozen construction document, unchanged)

| arm | condition | controls |
|---|---|---|
| A | budget-matched independent agents | same task attempts/compute |
| B | stable roles + fixed non-learned procedure | same roles and step allowance as D |
| C | raw admitted history, same evidence corpus | same record/token budget and role structure as D |
| D | compiled procedure from the same admitted corpus | same agents, inputs, allowed operations, work budget |

Fairness constraints carried over verbatim: the raw-history comparator
must be competent (same candidate evidence, same interpretation
opportunity); compilation cost is measured and assigned; no arm sees
evaluator truth; the World outcome law is treatment-blind.

## 3. What makes this a MECHANISM experiment, not a model test

- **The model never induces the procedure.** A deterministic compiler
  reads an admitted, evaluator-graded evidence index (SHA-bound) and
  selects between two opaque workflow orderings by graded success,
  abstaining on insufficient support. This is the construction
  doc's existing `ProcedureCompiler`, unchanged.
- **All model requests are EXECUTION-style** (apply a procedure /
  interpret a record / answer a task) — the only request class with a
  proven stable operating point in this program (128/128 parseable at
  High/4096 across the stepwise and semantic-clarity campaigns;
  420/420 in the G1 v9 pilot). No induction-grade reasoning anywhere.
  The 300-second wall documented in the closed acquisition line is
  structurally out of scope.
- **The model is a held-fixed component**: same model ID, effort,
  budget and temperature in every arm; membership and evidence corpus
  identical across arms; fresh-member execution isolates
  organization-owned knowledge from member memory.

## 4. Draft design parameters (to be powered and frozen before any run)

- Substrate: the existing two-step workflow task family from the G2
  construction (opaque context → two candidate orderings, graded by an
  independent evaluator on held-out operands). Schema diversity and
  task count to be set by a power calculation at freeze time; the
  construction doc's numeric defaults are explicitly smoke floors and
  are NOT sample-size justification.
- Unit of randomization: organization (matched roster), not task; tasks
  from one organization are correlated and are never counted as
  independent units (construction doc failure mode).
- Pre-declared gate logic: D vs C is the primary contrast; B and A are
  mechanism/usefulness controls; the removal condition is required for
  any causal attribution to the procedure itself. Abstention behavior
  of the compiler is reported, never silently treated as success.
- Turnover (Organization→Institution) is deliberately OUT of this
  experiment's claim and reserved for the separately gated successor
  study (fresh members of fresh members), per the construction doc.
- Success/failure conditions, SESOI, multiplicity, stopping rules:
  frozen at preregistration time per `mechanism-governance-v0.1` —
  this proposal deliberately does NOT pre-commit numbers.

## 5. Predictions that discriminate (registered at freeze, not now)

- If D > C materially and removal erases the gain: organizations can
  own compiled knowledge beyond what members extract from identical
  evidence — first positive Organization-layer mechanism over raw
  evidence.
- If D ≈ C: the H8 pattern generalizes to the organizational setting —
  representation/compilation adds nothing over competent raw-evidence
  use, and the program should stop building layers over raw history
  and test turnover of the RAW channel instead.
- If D < C or B ≈ D: compilation is not the mechanism; roles alone
  suffice; document and close.

All three outcomes are informative; none requires rescuing.
