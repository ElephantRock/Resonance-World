# D. Research Realignment Decision (2026-10-28)

Status: **decision record** prepared under the Research Program
Realignment directive. Zero live sends; zero budget; no historical
record modified.

## 1. The correction being adopted

The program's center of gravity returns to the architectural
transitions — **Models → Agents → Agent Organizations → Autonomous
Institutions** — measuring the causal increment each layer adds with
the model held fixed (Δ_Agent, Δ_Organization, Δ_Institution as paired
contrasts under matched information, resources and tasks).

GLM-5.3 (and any model) is hereby an experimental COMPONENT and a held
fixed variable, not a research subject. Model-capability findings
(acquisition, induction, wording, budgets, timeouts) are evidence about
a cognitive interface at tested operating points — valuable, retained,
and out of the critical path of architectural claims.

## 5. Roadmap effect

- The previous implicit gate "no C3R-G2 before G1 acquisition success"
  is **dissolved**. Causal dependency is asserted only inside an
  experiment where a mechanism truly needs its prerequisite (the
  successor study needs the primary contrast; nothing else does).
- Priority order for the next experimental investment, from the gap
  analysis: (1) organization-owned knowledge vs equal-information raw
  evidence (deliverable C), (2) turnover/regeneration of whatever
  channel wins, (3) composition beyond intact pairs. Model-baseline
  questions re-enter only as cross-model replication of an established
  architectural effect.
- Operating-point doctrine going forward: experiments must be built
  from request classes already proven stable at the registered
  operating point (execution-style requests), or must qualify their
  request class first with a bounded, campaign-gating probe — the
  pattern that correctly stopped the v2 acquisition attempt at one
  send instead of ninety-six.

## 6. Honest boundaries of this decision

- No architectural claim is promoted by this document; the evidence map
  records strengths as they are (mostly exploratory/confirmatory within
  single substrates; nothing at integration-eligible level under
  mechanism-governance-v0.1).
- Where evidence was unavailable (Piano phases not present in the
  working clone before fetch; acceptance branches carrying judgments
  rather than re-executions; D-line transport instabilities), the map
  says so rather than inferring outcomes.
- This decision binds program direction, not history: every prior
  RESULT keeps its recorded scope and immutability.
